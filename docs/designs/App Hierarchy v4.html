v4 · LIFECYCLE LOCKED

A self-hosted Offbook serves one family (or extended family). The instance hosts multiple users and multiple households, with each user belonging to at most one household. Visibility lives at the account level (3 levels). Members can leave on their own; their shared data is preserved for a grace period so a rejoin auto-resumes the relationship. The AI shares context across the household but chat threads are private by default — individually shareable.

What we locked in

11 answers across two sessions. The product model and its lifecycle are now fully specified.

Session 1 · the model
1 · Visibility scope Per-account, not per-transaction. Sharing an account brings its transactions along.
2 · Sharing default Private. Nothing enters a household until the account owner opts the account in.
3 · Architecture One instance, many users & many households. Each user belongs to at most one household.
4 · Roles Three: owner · contributor · view-only.
5 · AI advisor Lives in both scopes. Household AI sees only aggregates of opted-in accounts.
6 · Scope switcher Top of sidebar. Binary toggle. Defaults to Household at login when present.
Session 2 · lifecycle & ops
7 · Sign-up Instance admin picks one mode: local multi-tenant (anyone on the box can create a user) or invite-only.
8 · Leaving Self-service. A member can leave their household on their own — no owner approval.
9 · Grace period Household never auto-dissolves. Shared data is preserved X days after a member leaves so they can rejoin without losing history.
10 · Dangling links Kept but ignored while gone. If they rejoin, prior links to aggregated view / budgets / goals auto-resume.
11 · AI chat history Shared context, private threads. Each member's chat history is private by default; share individual threads to the household.

06

Member lifecycle — leave, grace, rejoin

Members can leave on their own. Shared data is preserved for a grace period so a rejoin restores the relationship without a re-setup. The household never auto-dissolves.

phase 1

Active member

Account shares are live. Contributions flow into household budgets & goals. AI context includes their aggregates.

→
phase 2

Leaves voluntarily

Settings → Household membership → Leave. One click. No owner approval. Confirms with grace-period note.

→
phase 3 · grace

Frozen links · X days

Account links preserved but ignored. They're excluded from aggregates today but the relationships remain on disk. Member sees they're not in any household.

→
phase 4a

Rejoins → auto-resume

Re-accept invite · prior account links, budget participations, goal splits, and shared threads reactivate automatically. No re-setup.

phase 4b

Grace expires

Links purged. Their past contributions stay in historical aggregates (read-only) so the household's history isn't rewritten.

Owner-side surface A small "In grace period" group at the bottom of the Members table. Owner can shorten or end the grace period for an individual member if desired.
Member-side surface After leaving, the personal sidebar loses the Household scope. A small banner: "You can rejoin Smith Household until Mar 12."
Grace duration Set by the household owner in Settings. Default suggestion: 30 days. Open question for you below.

First-run flow — instance, user, household

An instance is provisioned once. Each user then signs up & lands on their first scope. Joining a household is a separate (optional) leg.

step 0 · NEW

Sign in

First boot: admin user created & picks sign-up mode — local multi-tenant or invite-only. Subsequent users log in (or accept an invite).

→
step 1

Welcome

Your data stays on your machine. Pick a path: solo, start a household, or join one.

→
step 2 · NEW

Household

If starting: name it, invite by handle.
If joining: paste invite code, confirm role.
If solo: skip.

→
step 3

Add accounts

Plaid sandbox or CSV/OFX drop. Lands in your personal book — private by default.

→
step 4

Share accounts?

Per-account toggle: private · balance only · balance + txns. Skippable.

→
step 5

Confirm categories

Auto-categorize runs. Confirm top 20. Saves rules silently.

→
step 6

Set budgets

Personal envelopes from your spend. If household: suggest shared envelopes too. AI keys optional after.

What's left →
Information architecture is done. Open items are parameters, not structural:
• What's the default grace period? (30d? 60d? 90d?)
• When a member leaves, are their past contributions to shared budgets/goals frozen in place, or scrubbed from the history?
• Can an owner be the last contributor and dissolve? Or does ownership transfer first?
• Is the visibility "balance only" tier worth the cognitive cost, or do we collapse to two states (private / shared)?
